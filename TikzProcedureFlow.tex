\caption{MATH-3D-SCPTOP end to end. Columns keep being generated
\emph{after} the branching decisions, which a price-and-branch scheme
over a frozen pool cannot do. Two conventions are worth reading off the
diagram: the incumbent comes from an integer master solved
periodically over the pool rather than from the tree reaching
integrality, and the bound is decided at a single point --- either an
exact sweep proves that nothing prices out anywhere, or no bound is
reported at all.}

\section{Implementation and reproducibility}
\label{appendix:impl}

The method is implemented in Julia with the JuMP modelling layer, and
every model it solves --- masters, the construction's programmes,
pricing and placeability models --- is solved with Gurobi 13.0; the
compact reference models were solved with Gurobi 12.0. A run follows
Algorithm~\ref{alg:bp3d}: root column generation within a share of the
budget, the volumetric certificate, the root construction, then the
tree until a reserve in which the open regions are certified. A
placeability query tries the shelf design first and the exact test of
Section~\ref{sec:geo3d} second, and its verdict is kept for the run.
Every 3D plan is verified truck by truck before it is reported, and
every certified bound came back with every pair proved. Each instance
was solved once in each set, every job alone on its cores. Every run outside the
benchmark used one workstation (AMD Ryzen 5 5600X); the benchmark,
method and compact model alike, was drawn from one queue by three
machines: that workstation, a laptop (AMD Ryzen 7 260) and a
pinned 12-CPU block of a shared server under WSL2. Its budgets are
$900$ s of the workstation, converted by a fixed-work calibration that
times one Gurobi model at ten threads and one single-thread Julia code
on each machine: $720$ s for the method and $670$ s for the compact
model on the laptop, and $2\,700$ s for both on the server, a factor of
three where the calibration measured $3.4$ and $3.8$. Only the budget
and step~4's limit, a sixth of it, scale with the machine; the per-call
limits of Table~\ref{tab:impl} do not. The deadline is checked between
phases, so four method runs end $0.01$ to $1.47$ s past their budget,
and a compact run's wall clock includes building its model, up to
$29.6$ s past the solver's limit. Since the phases run on wall-clock
budgets, a run depends on the build, the machine and its load, and a
difference of one or two trucks between rows is not read as an
effect. Table~\ref{tab:impl} lists the settings; code and data:
